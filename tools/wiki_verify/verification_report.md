## Conflict (not resolved)

- **Detection risk**: the ISACA glossary says detection risk is the failure of the *enterprise's internal controls* to detect errors or fraud in time. The wiki, the Sybex guide (p1:4555) and auditing standards say it is the risk that the *auditor's procedures* fail to detect a material error.
